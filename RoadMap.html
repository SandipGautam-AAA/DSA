🚀 Modern Era Success Strategies

Patterns over Problems

Don't memorize 500 individual solutions. Master **Patterns** (Sliding Window, Two Pointers). One pattern solves 50+ problems.

C (Logic) + C++ (Speed)

Write the data structure in **C** once to understand pointers, then use **C++ STL** for solving problems fast. Companies care about logic, but they hire for speed and C++ knowledge.

Space-Time Complexity Tradeoffs

Always ask: "Can I make this faster by using more memory?" This is the core of Hashing and Dynamic Programming.
